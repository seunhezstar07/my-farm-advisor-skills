# eda-assignment2

Custom EDA subskill comparing field boundaries, CDL/cropland data, weather, and soil properties across IL, IA, and NE growers.

| Grower | County | State | Fields | Total area (ac.) | Mean field (ac.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `il-grower` | Iroquois | IL | 10 | 804 | 80 |
| `ia-grower` | Kossuth | IA | 10 | 1,186 | 119 |
| `ne-grower` | Perkins | NE | 10 | 3,600 | 360 |

Generates 13 static matplotlib PNGs under `growers/eda/assignment2/plots/` and a self-contained HTML report at `growers/eda/assignment2/report/assignment2_eda_report.html`.
